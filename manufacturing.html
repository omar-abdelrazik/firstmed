How a Right Pro dressing is made.

Our first line is the clearest way to show what we do. Below is the process, step by step, followed by photos taken on the factory floor.

From roll to sealed pouch.

Five steps, the same for every size from 6 × 8 to 10 × 35 cm.

The process

  1. Materials on reels

    The non-woven fabric with its adhesive layer, the absorbent pad material and the protective release paper are loaded on reels.

  2. Pad placed

    The absorbent pad is cut and laid on the adhesive side, leaving a clean adhesive border all round.

  3. Cut to size

    Each dressing is cut to its final size, from 6 × 8 up to 10 × 35 cm.

  4. Sealed and dated

    Every dressing is sealed in its own pouch, printed with its production and expiry dates.

  5. Packed

    Pouches go into cartons of 20, or 75 for the 6 × 8 cm size, labelled in Arabic and English.
