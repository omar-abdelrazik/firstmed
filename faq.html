Questions buyers ask us

Straight answers, including the ones that aren't flattering.

Are you a manufacturer or a trader?

A manufacturer. Our factory is in Tala, Menoufia, and that is where Right Pro dressings are made.

How long have you been operating?

The factory was founded in 2022. Our partners have more than 30 years in the medical-supplies industry.

Do you export?

Not yet. The Gulf and Europe are our target for 2027. If you're a distributor in those markets, we'd like to talk now.

Which certificates and registrations do you hold?

Tell us which product you're interested in and we'll send its current documents. We don't list certificates on this site, because what matters is the paperwork for your product in your market.

What is your minimum order?

It depends on the product and the packaging. You'll get an exact figure in your quote.

Can you make products under our brand?

Yes. See how private label works.

Can we get samples?

Ask us with the product and size you need, and we'll tell you what we can send.

What should a quote request include?

Product, size, quantity, delivery city or port, and any documents your buyer or tender requires.

Can we visit the factory?

Yes. If you're evaluating us as a supplier, contact us to arrange a visit.

Why is there less on this site than on bigger manufacturers’ sites?

Because we only publish what is true today. As the factory grows, this site will grow with it.

Have a question that isn’t here?

Ask us directly. You will get a direct answer.
