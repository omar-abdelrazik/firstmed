A new factory. Partners with decades in the trade.

First Medical was founded in 2022 in Tala, Menoufia, by partners who have spent more than 30 years in the medical-supplies industry. We make medical consumables, starting with adhesive wound dressings, and we're building the factory, its systems and its range one step at a time.

Vision

Reliable medical quality within reach of every healthcare provider, and "Made in Egypt" as a reason to choose a product, not a reason to discount it.

Mission

To make dependable medical consumables at a fair cost, build the systems behind them, and reinvest what we earn in capacity, people and new markets.

What we're building toward

Four ideas sit behind every decision we make, from what we price to which line we add next.

Impact الأثر

We don't want a business that only makes a profit. We want the work to be worth something to the people who use it.

  • Medical supplies of reliable quality at a sensible cost.
  • Helping hospitals and care providers get the products they need.
  • Narrowing the gap between what a medical product costs and how good it is.
  • Better local availability of everyday supplies.

Accessibility إتاحة الرعاية الصحية

Not every hospital has the same budget, and not every patient can carry the cost of care. We're building a company on the idea that reliable medical quality doesn't have to be expensive.

Industrial excellence التميز الصناعي

We're small today, and we don't intend to stay a small workshop with a handful of items.

  • Better manufacturing processes and higher quality standards.
  • Real systems for operations, management and sales.
  • New production lines and new products.
  • Gulf and European markets.
  • Egyptian manufacturing as a competitive edge.

Sustainable growth النمو المستدام

Not expansion for its own sake. A business that earns a real return and reinvests it in production capacity, jobs and new markets. Impact without a sound business model rarely lasts.

So far, and next

Outlined years are targets, not achievements.

  1. 2022

    The factory is founded in Tala, Menoufia.

  2. Today

How we talk about ourselves

Four rules for this website, our quotes and every conversation with a buyer.

  • We only publish numbers we can prove.
  • Goals are labelled as goals, never as achievements.
  • If we can't supply something, we say so early.
  • We send real documents for the product you're buying, and never claim a certificate we don't hold.
