## Rules for both languages

- **Specific beats impressive.** Write "five sizes, 6 × 8 to 10 × 35 cm", not "a wide range".
- **Facts are facts and goals are goals.** "2027" is always marked as a target.
- **Say no early.** "If we can't supply it, we'll say so" is a selling point.
- **No superlatives:** leading, world-class, premium, best, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge.
- **Short sentences, active verbs, "we" and "you".**

## Writing Arabic (write it first, don't translate it)

- Use simplified MSA (فصحى مبسطة) that reads naturally in Egypt and the Gulf. No slang: write «تريد» instead of «تحب».
- Avoid filler that sounds generated: «نسعى جاهدين»، «رائدة»، «حلول متكاملة»، «بأعلى معايير الجودة»، «رحلتنا»، «شريككم الأمثل»، «نفخر بـ»، «في عالم يتسارع».
- Don't translate idioms. Write «ونتولى نحن الباقي» instead of «ونحن نكمل من هناك».
- Use Western digits (1، 2، 3) for sizes, prices, phone numbers and dates, matching the packaging.
- In lists, keep the Arabic and English facts the same, but let each sentence be built the way its own language builds it.
