# Brand voice: First Medical / 1st MED

**Tagline:** Making quality accessible. / جودة طبية في متناول الجميع.

## How we sound

We sound like a confident manufacturer: specific about what we make, and plain about our plans. We state facts and let the products do the talking. We don't comment on our own honesty or on the website itself.

## Four pillars (the message behind every page)

| Pillar | English | العربية |

## Rules for both languages

- **Specific beats impressive.** Write "five sizes, 6 × 8 to 10 × 35 cm", not "a wide range".
- **Targets are written as targets:** "targeted for 2027", never as achievements.
- **No superlatives:** leading, world-class, premium, best, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge.
- **No meta-commentary:** never write about the site, about how honest we are, or what we "won't claim".
- **Never publish internal ambitions as weaknesses** ("we're small", "a small workshop").
- **Short sentences, active verbs, "we" and "you".**

## Writing Arabic (write it first, don't translate it)

- Every word on an Arabic page is Arabic. Never place the English term next to an Arabic title. The only exceptions are brand marks (CE), email addresses and the language switch.
- Use simplified MSA (فصحى مبسطة) that reads naturally in Egypt and the Gulf. No slang.
- Avoid filler that sounds generated: «نسعى جاهدين»، «رائدة»، «حلول متكاملة»، «بأعلى معايير الجودة»، «رحلتنا»، «شريككم الأمثل»، «نفخر بـ».
- Don't translate idioms. Say the thing the Arabic way.
- Use Western digits (1، 2، 3), as the packaging does. Let sizes flow right to left: «10 × 18 سم».
