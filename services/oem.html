OEM and private label

Right Pro comes in its own printed pouches and cartons. Your product can too.

What you can customise

  • Your brand and artwork on the pouch.
  • Your carton design, and the languages on it.
  • The number of pieces per carton.
  • Sizes within our range, from 6 × 8 to 10 × 35 cm. Ask about others.
