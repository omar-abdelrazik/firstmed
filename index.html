About The Company

Engineering reliability into every medical consumable we make

First Medical Factory is a specialist manufacturer of wound care and surgical consumables. From a single production line to a multi-line, export-ready facility, we have grown by holding one principle constant: the products we ship protect patients — so quality is never optional.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted regional manufacturing partner for medical consumables, recognised globally for quality, consistency and supply reliability.

Our Mission

To manufacture safe, effective and affordable medical consumables at scale — empowering healthcare providers, distributors and governments to deliver better care.

How We Manufacture

A controlled journey from raw material to your dock

Nine disciplined stages, each with defined controls, checkpoints and records.

Raw Materials

Medical-grade nonwovens, gauze and adhesives sourced from qualified, audited suppliers.

Incoming QC

Every consignment tested and quarantined until cleared against material specifications.

Production

Automated cutting, folding and assembly under controlled-environment conditions.

In-Process Inspection

Line operators and QC perform continuous checks against tolerances and SPC limits.

Sterilization

Validated sterilization with documented cycle records and sterility assurance.

Packaging

Primary & secondary packaging with lot coding for full traceability.

Final QC

Finished-goods release testing and documentation review before any batch ships.

Warehousing

Released stock stored in inventory-managed warehouse, ready for rapid order fulfilment.

Distribution

Domestic and export logistics coordinated with complete shipping documentation.

FAQ

Everything procurement teams ask

What is your typical lead time?

Standard catalog products typically ship within 10–15 working days of a confirmed order. Custom and private-label orders depend on artwork and volume — we confirm an exact schedule with every quotation.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product and packaging. Standard items start at carton-level quantities, while private-label runs have higher minimums. Exact MOQs are listed on each product and in your quotation.

Do you export internationally?

Yes. We export to 30+ countries and handle full export documentation, regulatory dossiers and freight coordination (FOB, CIF and other Incoterms available).

Can you customise products and packaging?

Absolutely. We offer OEM and private-label services including custom sizes, materials, branding, multilingual labelling and bespoke packaging configurations.

What certifications do you hold?

Our facility operates under ISO 13485 and ISO 9001 with CE marking, GMP-aligned practices and relevant national regulatory approvals. Certificates are available on request.

What payment terms do you offer?

We work with T/T and L/C for export orders, with terms tailored to order size and relationship. Specific terms are agreed at the quotation stage.

How do you handle shipping & logistics?

We coordinate sea and air freight through trusted partners, provide all shipping documentation, and can ship under your preferred Incoterms to your nominated port or door.
